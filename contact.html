Shipping & Returns

🚚 Shipping

We ship to the US and Canada. Orders are carefully packed with eco-friendly materials and typically depart within 2-3 business days. Tracking information is provided via email once your order ships.

↩️ Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition. Contact us to initiate a return, and we'll provide a prepaid shipping label.

🛡️ Guarantee

All terrariums come with a 6-month quality guarantee. If your ecosystem arrives damaged or fails to thrive under proper care, we'll replace it at no cost.

Quick Answers

Common questions by category.

🌿 Ordering

How long to process?

Orders typically ship within 2-3 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking once your terrarium is on its way.

Can I modify order?

Contact us within 24 hours of placing your order. We'll do our best to accommodate changes before production begins.

🚚 Shipping

Where do you ship?

We currently ship to all 50 US states and Canada. International shipping may be available—contact us to inquire.

Is packaging eco-friendly?

Yes! All our packaging is 100% recyclable—paper cushioning, cardboard boxes, and no plastic wrap.

💧 Care

Need replacement plants?

If your plants fail to thrive within 6 months under proper care, contact us for a replacement or repair options.

What if mold appears?

Mold is usually harmless. Wipe it away with a damp cloth and ensure your terrarium gets adequate indirect light.

🎨 Custom

How long does custom take?

Custom terrariums typically take 2-3 weeks from consultation to delivery, depending on complexity and availability.

What's the minimum budget?

Custom designs start at $100. Most projects fall between $150-$300 depending on size and plant selection.
