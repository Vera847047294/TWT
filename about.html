Our Story

Bringing the healing power of nature to urban spaces, one jar at a time.

Why We Started

In the heart of the city, surrounded by concrete and screens, many of us have lost touch with the natural world. We spend our days in offices with recycled air and artificial light, returning to apartments where a wilting houseplant serves as our only green companion—and a guilt-ridden reminder of our failed attempts at nurturing life.

Terra Microscape was born from this disconnect. We believed there had to be a better way—a way for urban professionals and students to experience the calming presence of living plants without the anxiety of constant care. The answer came in the form of a simple glass jar.

Closed terrariums are miniature ecosystems, self-sustaining worlds that recycle their own water and nutrients. They bring the tranquility of a forest hike to your desk, the serenity of a rainforest to your bedroom—all with virtually no maintenance. Our mission is to make this magic accessible to everyone, regardless of space, time, or prior plant experience.

Every terrarium we craft is an invitation to pause, breathe, and remember that we're part of something larger. A tiny forest on your desk is more than decor—it's a daily reminder of the natural world waiting just beyond our doors.

Meet the Founder

Alex Rivera

Landscape Designer & Plant Enthusiast

"I believe everyone deserves a piece of nature in their space, no matter how small. A terrarium isn't just a plant—it's a living work of art that asks nothing of you but to be appreciated. My goal is to help people rediscover their connection to the natural world, one tiny forest at a time."

Our Design Philosophy

Three principles guide every terrarium we create.

🌿

Nature

We let nature lead. Each terrarium mimics real ecosystems, using authentic plant combinations and organic materials. No artificial elements—just genuine living landscapes.

💚

Healing

Plants have proven therapeutic benefits—reduced stress, improved focus, increased calm. Our terrariums are designed not just to survive, but to contribute to your wellbeing.

♻️

Sustainability

A closed terrarium is the ultimate sustainable plant. Once established, it needs no external resources—no watering, no fertilizers, no plastic pots destined for landfills.

Our Promise to the Earth

Every order plants a seed in the city – we donate $1 per jar to urban community greening projects.

All our packaging is 100% recyclable. From the protective boxes to the paper cushioning, every material is chosen with the planet in mind.

Our Journey

From a single idea to thousands of happy customers.

2020

Idea Born

The concept of desk-friendly terrariums takes shape after countless conversations with stressed, plant-killing friends.

2021

First Prototype

After months of experimentation, the perfect self-sustaining ecosystem is achieved in a small Brooklyn apartment.

2022

Online Launch

Terra Microscape opens its virtual doors, shipping handcrafted terrariums to customers across the US.

2023

10,000 Jars Sold

A milestone worth celebrating. Each sale represents $1 donated to urban greening and one more tiny forest in the world.
